2. Information Collected

The App does not currently use a separate third-party analytics service. However, third-party SDKs integrated into the App may automatically collect certain information.

The App uses Google AdMob to display rewarded advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or share information including:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Device and advertising identifiers.
  • App interactions, such as ad views, taps and video interactions.
  • Diagnostic information and performance data.
  • Advertising-related information.

This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, measurement and service improvement.

Advertising personalization and data processing are subject to applicable privacy laws, user settings and child-directed advertising requirements.

Google Play Games Services

Google Play Games sign-in is optional. You can play the game without signing in.

If you choose to sign in, Google Play Games may process information associated with your gaming profile, achievements, leaderboard scores and game activity.

This information is handled according to Google's privacy policy and Google Play Games terms.
